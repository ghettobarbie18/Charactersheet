WHAT DOESN'T KILL ME MIGHT MAKE ME KILL YOU.
Important Notes: In case anyone is confused on this family, well let's just say they are pretty much a dime a dozen. Considering Demo's father raised them up in a very harrowing fashion, some of the kiddos are a bit crazy and some even developed a taste for their own kind - which is what brings me to the cannibalism side of the family. Dark and grueling, I know, but it's an important factor if you're wanting to make a Skulgarth. Demonetta does NOT practice cannibalism, even though she was brought up around it and forced to feast upon her own kind, she does not condone it whatsoever. But that shouldn't stop you from making one of her brothers (or another family member) a cannibal. It's entirely up to you. Another thing to note is that if you wish for your character to be pulled more towards the religious side, the main god the Skulgarth's believe in is Jezelda, however they do often mention a demon quite often, the Wendigo who embodies death and cannibalism. More often than not, if you choose for your character to be religious, they could take on ritualistic tendencies which is sacrificing other animals and/or family members to the Wendigo beneath a full moon to also show respect for Jezelda. But all of this is completely optional.

Family Alignment: Between Lawful evil, Neutral Evil, and Chaotic Evil. But some members are Chaotic Good.

Religion: Religious Cult, beliefs vary, though they worship the Goddess Jezelda who is considered the "Mistress of the Hungry Moon". Though worshiping is optional (as Demo doesn't worship any God her family believes in).

Appearances: This is pretty open, looks and attributes can go in any direction. So feel free to be as creative as you like with designs! There is one design in particular I'm willing to part with in exchange for a deluxe character pass OR a mutation pass.

Naming Theme: I don't necessarily have a naming theme, I'd prefer for her brothers names to stay the same but if you wish to change the alias that is fine with me! The names should be a darker tone, or even demon god names - up to you!
TO CONSIDER:

✦ Skills that this family mainly partner with are FIGHTING but you are more than welcome to have any other skill or to have them not be a fighter at all, that's ultimately up to you!
